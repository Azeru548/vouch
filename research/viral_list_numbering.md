# The viral list — recovered numbering and coverage

The list circulating in Oct 2026 as *"NAFDAC ALERT: Scurious people are faking these 149 products in Nigeria"* exists in **at least two numbering variants** (a full 149-item one and a compressed ~120-item one). Everything below is the **full 149-item variant**, pinned by cross-checking four independent reposts against the four screenshots supplied.

⚠️ **Reposts disagree with each other.** One compressed variant reads "Seretide Accuhaler 71. Tarivid, Embacef & Wonder Calcium 72" — grouping three products into one line. Do not treat any single repost's numbers as canonical.

## Coverage

- **Recovered: items 1–118** (117 transcribed + item 76 missing), i.e. **~79% of the list.**
- **Not recovered: items 119–149** (~31 items).
- Search snippets could not go past 120 — Facebook and Instagram block page reads, and the snippets cap at the same anchors. Recovering the tail needs the original screenshots.

## Why the tail matters less than it looks

The four tail items we *can* identify are all **already in our database**:

| Item | Our alert |
|---|---|
| Dex Luxury Bar Soap | `012/2024` (BMHCA ban) |
| Nivea Roll-On Deodorant | `041/2024` (EU RAPEX) |
| Fair Child Soap | `20/2025` |
| Brown Henna Hair Colour | `33/2023` |

Items 1–118 are the *contested, uncited, non-alert* part of the list — which is exactly why today's research concentrated there. The unrecovered tail runs straight through the cosmetic/hair alerts NAFDAC published as formal numbered alerts in 2023–2025, all of which our sync already covers.
