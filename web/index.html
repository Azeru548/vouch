Confirm the product

Enter the product name exactly as printed on the pack. The registration number is optional.

Scan it from the pack or type it. No number printed? Leave this blank — food, drinks and cosmetics often have none, and the name and photo are still checked against known fakes.
PPB recalls are batch-specific, and the batch code (BATCH, LOT or B.No) is printed on every blister and label — often the only readable number on the pack.

Know the limit of the check. Vouch compares your details with a local registry snapshot. A result does not replace professional medical advice or guarantee the product’s quality.

Your reports protect others. If a pack looks wrong, file a report after your check — after 3 reports about the same product in 30 days, everyone who checks it sees a warning.
